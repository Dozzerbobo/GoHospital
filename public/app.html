Get the GoHospital app

Find the right hospital in one tap — with live emergency status, saved for quick access on your phone.

Download for Android (APK)

Install on Android

  1. Open gohospital.online in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app above, or Chrome menu ⋮ → Install app.
  3. Open GoHospital from your home screen.

Using the APK file? Allow “install unknown apps” for your browser when Android asks, then open the downloaded file.

Install on iPhone

  1. Open gohospital.online in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
